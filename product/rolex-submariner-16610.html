About this watch

The last of the classic aluminium-bezel Submariner Dates, and for many collectors the purest. The 16610 ran from 1988 to 2010 with the cal. 3135, a sapphire crystal and the slimmer case that later ceramic models gave up. This one wears its twenty years honestly: a clean dial and hands, a bezel insert without fade, and a case that has seen one careful polish.
