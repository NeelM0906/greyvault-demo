FAQ

Straight answers

Are your watches authentic?

Yes. Every piece is opened and inspected by our watchmaker before it is listed, and every sale carries a lifetime authenticity guarantee. If any watch we sold is ever shown not to be genuine, we refund the full purchase price.

What does “grey market” mean?

Grey market means the watch is sold by an independent dealer, not an authorized retailer. The watches are genuine. It is how most pre-owned and hard-to-find references are bought. Manufacturer warranties carry over where still active.

Why do some watches say “Price on request”?

Prices on a few high-demand references move week to week. We would rather give you a firm, current number by message than show a stale one. Ask and we answer the same day.

Can I hold a watch?

Yes. Use “Hold 24 hours” on any listing. A hold is free and gives you time to see it or arrange payment. After 24 hours it is released unless you ask us to extend.

Do you accept trade-ins?

Yes. Put any watch you own toward a piece on the site. Send photos through the Sell or trade form and we quote within one business day.

How do I pay?

Bank wire is preferred and has no fee. We also accept certified checks, cash in person, cards up to $15,000 with a 3% fee, and financing through a lending partner subject to approval.

How does shipping work?

Free insured overnight shipping in the US. FedEx Priority, signature required, fully insured door to door. International shipping on request, insured, duties paid by the buyer.

What is your return policy?

You have 7 days from delivery to return a watch in the same condition it left us, with all included items and the security tag intact, for a full refund.

What does the warranty cover?

2 years on the movement and its function. It excludes crystals, straps, water damage after delivery and accidental damage. Warranty work is done in house.

Can I visit?

Yes. We are at 47 West 47th Street, Booth 12, New York. Walk-ins are welcome during opening hours. Book an appointment if you want a specific watch ready and a private table.
