How we authenticate

Six checks before a watch is listed

The street's reputation for fakes, swapped parts and polished cases is earned by a few. We answer it with a process you can read, and a report signed by the person who did the work.

Read the full process
  1. 01

    Source

    We buy from owners and collectors we can identify. Every seller shows ID and signs a statement of ownership. Serials are checked against stolen-watch registries before we pay.

  2. 02

    Open

    A watchmaker removes the case back and inspects the movement, calibre number, rotor, bridges and engravings against manufacturer references for that serial range.

  3. 03

    Measure

    Timegrapher readings in six positions: rate, amplitude and beat error. Water resistance pressure tested. Results go on the listing, not in a drawer.

  4. 04

    Inspect

    Case, bezel, dial, hands, crystal and bracelet checked under 10x magnification for aftermarket parts, refinished dials and relumed hands. Any polishing is disclosed.

  5. 05

    Document

    Condition report written and signed by the inspecting watchmaker. Photographs of every flaw we find. You see exactly what we saw.

  6. 06

    Guarantee

    Every watch ships with our lifetime authenticity guarantee and a 2-year mechanical warranty, transferable to the next owner.
