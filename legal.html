Terms of sale

All watches are pre-owned unless described as unworn. GreyVault Timepieces LLC is an independent dealer and is not affiliated with or authorized by any manufacturer named on this site. Prices are in US dollars and exclude applicable sales tax. A sale is final once funds clear and the watch is delivered, subject to the return policy below.

Warranty

Every watch carries a 2-year mechanical warranty from the date of delivery covering the movement and its function. It excludes crystals, straps, gaskets after the first year, water damage after delivery, and accidental or impact damage. Warranty service is performed in house. The warranty transfers to a subsequent owner.

Authenticity guarantee

If any watch sold by us is shown by the manufacturer or a recognized independent authority not to be genuine, or to contain parts not disclosed at sale, we refund the full purchase price on return of the watch. No time limit.

Returns

Return any watch within 7 days of delivery in the condition it left us, with all included items and the security tag intact, for a full refund to the original payment method. Return shipping is insured and prepaid by us within the US. Sized bracelets with removed links must include all links.

Holds and offers

A hold reserves a watch for 24 hours without payment. Offers are answered within one business day. An accepted offer is held for 24 hours pending payment.

Privacy

We collect the details you send through forms, WhatsApp, email or phone in order to answer you and complete a sale. We do not sell or share personal information with third parties except carriers, payment processors and lenders needed to complete your transaction. Saved watches are stored only in your browser. This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. To ask for your data to be deleted, email desk@greyvault.example.

Trademarks

Rolex, Audemars Piguet, Patek Philippe, Omega, Cartier, Tudor and all model names are trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to describe the watches offered.
