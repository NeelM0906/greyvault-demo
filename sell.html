Sell or trade

A firm offer within one business day

Send the reference, a few photos and what comes with it. We reply with one number to buy outright and one to trade toward any piece in our inventory. No obligation.

  1. 01

    Tell us about it

    Reference, year, box and papers, condition. Photos of the dial, case back and clasp help us quote accurately.

  2. 02

    Get two numbers

    An outright purchase price and a trade value. Trade values run higher because the watch stays with us.

  3. 03

    Bring it in or ship it

    Visit the booth or ship with our prepaid, fully insured label. The watchmaker confirms condition in front of you or on video.

  4. 04

    Get paid the same day

    Wire, certified check or a trade toward your next watch. We pay off any outstanding finance directly.
