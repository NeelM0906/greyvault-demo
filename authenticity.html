Authenticity

What happens before a watch is listed

Buyers on 47th Street worry about counterfeits, mixed parts, refinished dials and over-polished cases. These are the checks we do so you do not have to.

  1. 01

    Source

    We buy from owners and collectors we can identify. Every seller shows ID and signs a statement of ownership. Serials are checked against stolen-watch registries before we pay.

  2. 02

    Open

    A watchmaker removes the case back and inspects the movement, calibre number, rotor, bridges and engravings against manufacturer references for that serial range.

  3. 03

    Measure

    Timegrapher readings in six positions: rate, amplitude and beat error. Water resistance pressure tested. Results go on the listing, not in a drawer.

  4. 04

    Inspect

    Case, bezel, dial, hands, crystal and bracelet checked under 10x magnification for aftermarket parts, refinished dials and relumed hands. Any polishing is disclosed.

  5. 05

    Document

    Condition report written and signed by the inspecting watchmaker. Photographs of every flaw we find. You see exactly what we saw.

  6. 06

    Guarantee

    Every watch ships with our lifetime authenticity guarantee and a 2-year mechanical warranty, transferable to the next owner.

Condition grading

Four grades, used consistently

Every listing states its grade and the specific flaws behind it. Polishing is always disclosed separately.

Unworn
Never worn. Factory stickers or seals where applicable. No marks under a 10x loupe.
Excellent
Light hairlines visible only under a loupe or at an angle. No dings, no dial or hand flaws.
Very good
Light desk-diving marks from careful wear. Nothing that affects function or the dial.
Good
Visible wear, softened edges or polish. Fully functional. Priced to reflect condition.

What we refuse

Watches we will not sell

  • Any watch with aftermarket parts we cannot fully disclose
  • Refinished or redialed watches presented as original
  • Watches without a verifiable owner
  • Serials that appear on any stolen-watch registry
  • Cases polished past the point of original geometry

The guarantee

Lifetime authenticity. 2‑year mechanical warranty.

If any watch we sold is ever shown not to be genuine, or to contain parts we did not disclose, we refund the full purchase price. No time limit, and it transfers with the watch.

Want a second opinion? We welcome inspection by any independent watchmaker or the manufacturer before or after purchase, and we will hold the watch while you arrange it.
